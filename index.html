How the Model Works
Price = 12.50 × 1.19PullCost × 1.41Desirability
Pull Cost (Supply)

Packs-per-hit × cards-in-tier ÷ 100. Each +1 → +19% price.

Desirability (Demand)

Character (45%) + Artwork (45%) + Appeal (10%). Each +1 → +41% price. Auto-calibrated from market data.

R² = 0.88

Explains 88% of price variation in modern chase cards.
