How the Model Works

Price = 12.50 × 1.19PullCost × 1.41Desirability
Pull Cost (Supply)

Measures scarcity. Calculated as packs-per-hit × cards-in-tier ÷ 100. Higher = harder to pull. Each +1 point raises predicted price by ~19%.

Desirability (Demand)

Weighted score: Character Premium (45%) + Artwork/Hype (45%) + Universal Appeal (10%). Each +1 point raises predicted price by ~41% — twice the impact of supply.

R² = 0.88

The model explains 88% of price variation in modern chase cards. Outliers exist due to hype cycles, new collector demand, and pack-opening aversion.
